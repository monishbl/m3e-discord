M3E Discord

Discord with rounded panels, playful shapes and chat bubbles. Pick a look and paste its link into Vencord. Then, if you like, use the box below to put your own messages on the right.

In Discord: Settings → Themes → Online Themes, then paste one link. One look at a time.

Your messages on the right

Discord doesn't tell themes who wrote what, so introduce yourself once with your user ID. Everything happens on this page; nothing gets sent anywhere.

  1. Discord → Settings → Advanced → turn on Developer Mode.
  2. Right-click your own name → Copy User ID, paste it here.
  3. Copy the snippet into Vencord → Settings → QuickCSS → Edit QuickCSS.

You'll need a profile picture (Discord's default ones don't carry your ID). Changed your mind? Just delete the snippet.
